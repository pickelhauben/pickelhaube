## My story

I'm a first-generation student who grew up between China and the United States. I arrived in Arizona closed off, and learned to lead by starting conversations first and joining everything I could.

Since then I've helped build a student association, co-founded a startup, and joined a global community of young leaders. Now I'm asking a bigger question: how do we build organizations that help people see the systems they live in?

Today I study economics and photography at the University of Arizona.
